Technical problems

The game freezes, crashes or does not load.

Close the Game completely and open it again, make sure your device has the latest FLAPPY SKY version from the App Store or Google Play, and restart your device. If the problem continues, write to us with your device model, operating system version and the steps that cause the problem.

How do I play?

Tap the screen to flap and fly between the pillars without touching them or the ground. Free Mode is an endless flight to beat your best score. Level Mode has 100 levels in 10 worlds, each with goals that give up to 3 stars.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. You can play offline. Ads, rewarded videos and purchases need an internet connection.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your progress is saved only on your device; there are no accounts. Uninstalling the Game or changing devices can erase coins, characters and other local progress, and we cannot recover them. Purchases are linked to your Apple or Google account and can be restored (see below).

Purchases and restoring Premium

What is FLAPPY SKY Premium?

An optional one-time purchase (not a subscription) that permanently removes the ads shown between games. Rewarded videos stay optional. Premium does not add coins or characters and does not unlock levels.

What is LEVEL PACK? Can I buy it?

LEVEL PACK is a planned optional one-time purchase that will unlock levels 31 to 100 of Level Mode. Levels 1 to 30 are free. LEVEL PACK is not available for purchase yet; in the Game it appears as "Available soon". This page will be updated when it becomes available.

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy, then open Settings → Premium and tap "RESTORE PURCHASES". Restoring is free.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play from your order history at play.google.com.

Ads and rewards

A rewarded video did not give me coins.

Coins are given only when the ad provider confirms the video was watched to the end. Rewarded videos are limited per day and may be unavailable if no ad can be loaded at that moment. Try again later.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → "Ad Privacy Choices" (shown where it applies in your region). The Game always requests non-personalized ads and does not track you.

Frequently asked questions

What does "Reset Progress" do?

It erases your best score, statistics, achievements, theme and Level Mode stars and progress. It keeps your coins, characters bought with coins, purchases, conquered worlds and trophies, and settings. See section 1 of the Privacy Policy for the full list.

How does the Lucky Spin work?

It is free once every 24 hours and gives virtual coins or, rarely, the character "Lucky". Spins cannot be bought.

How can I ask about or delete my data?

See our Privacy Policy and write to the email above.

¿Cómo se juega?

Toca la pantalla para aletear y vuela entre los pilares sin tocarlos ni tocar el suelo. El Modo Libre es un vuelo infinito para superar tu récord. El Modo Niveles tiene 100 niveles en 10 mundos, cada uno con objetivos que dan hasta 3 estrellas.

¿Necesito conexión a internet?

No. Puedes jugar sin conexión. Los anuncios, los videos con recompensa y las compras necesitan internet.

Tu progreso se guarda solo en tu dispositivo; no hay cuentas. Las compras están vinculadas a tu cuenta de Apple o Google y se pueden restaurar (ver más abajo).

Compras y restauración de Premium

¿Qué es FLAPPY SKY Premium?

Una compra única opcional (no es una suscripción) que elimina para siempre los anuncios entre partidas. Los videos con recompensa siguen siendo opcionales. Premium no añade monedas ni personajes y no desbloquea niveles.

¿Qué es LEVEL PACK? ¿Puedo comprarlo?

LEVEL PACK es una compra única opcional prevista que desbloqueará los niveles 31 al 100 del Modo Niveles. Los niveles 1 al 30 son gratuitos. LEVEL PACK todavía no está disponible para comprar; en el Juego aparece como "Available soon" (disponible pronto). Actualizaremos esta página cuando esté disponible.

¿Cómo restauro mis compras?

Asegúrate de usar el mismo Apple ID o la misma cuenta de Google con la que compraste. Luego abre Ajustes → Premium y pulsa "RESTORE PURCHASES" (Restaurar compras). Restaurar es gratis.

¿Cómo pido un reembolso?

Los pagos los procesan Apple o Google, por eso ellos gestionan los reembolsos: Apple en reportaproblem.apple.com y Google Play desde tu historial de pedidos en play.google.com.

Anuncios y recompensas

Un video con recompensa no me dio monedas.

Las monedas solo se entregan cuando el proveedor de anuncios confirma que el video se vio completo. Los videos con recompensa tienen un límite diario y pueden no estar disponibles si en ese momento no se puede cargar un anuncio. Inténtalo más tarde.

¿Cómo cambio mis opciones de privacidad de anuncios?

Abre Ajustes → "Ad Privacy Choices" (aparece donde aplica en tu región). El Juego siempre pide anuncios no personalizados y no te rastrea.
